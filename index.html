A DATA SORCERER AT MAS HOLDINGS

SLIIT graduate Nipuna Abeysekara bewitches the apparel giant with moving dashboards, enchanted machine-counts and self-writing reports.

OUR correspondent reports that a young data scientist has been quietly transforming the great manufacturing houses of the realm. Where others see spreadsheets, Mr Abeysekara sees prophecy — coaxing raw operational data into decisions with little more than Python, Power BI and a steady wand hand.

A Bachelor of Science (Honours) in Information Technology, specialising in Data Science, he graduated from the S.L.I.I.T. academy with a Second Class and promptly took up a post as Associate Data Scientist at MAS Holdings.

His first four moons were served in Twinery, the innovation arm, before he was called to the MCAP / Machine Build team — where, sources whisper, he conjures interactive dashboards for CapEx tracking, savings analysis and machine reconciliation across the production divisions.

“I turn messy data into clear, decision-ready insight — one enchanted dashboard at a time.”

Perhaps most curious is his reconciliation charm, which compares the inventory scrolls against physically-scanned machines to reveal those matched, missing and mismatched — a feat that has much improved machine governance across the factories.
